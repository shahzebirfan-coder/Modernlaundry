MODERN DRY CLEANERS — 7 NAYI CHEEZEIN (client ki request par)
==============================================================

1) PURANA BAQI (Previous Remaining Balance)
   Kahan: New Sale (POS) → cart ke neeche "📋 Purana Baqi" box
   • Register se purana baqi amount likh dein -> grand total me add ho jayega
   • Discount sirf is invoice ke bill par lagta hai, purane baqi par nahi
   • Jab customer ka naam/number enter karein aur us ka purana baqi mojood ho,
     booking form par RED nahi, bara sa reminder aata hai:
        "⚠️ Is customer ka PURANA BAQI Rs. X hai"  + button  ➕ Add to this invoice
   • Har invoice par save hota hai (currentBill + prevBalance) — ledger/reports sahi rehte hain
   • Receipt aur PDF par "Purana Baqi" line bhi aati hai

2) CONTACT NUMBER AB LAZMI NAHI
   • Booking form me "Contact Number (optional)"
   • Number na ho to customer ka naam se match hota hai (duplicate na banta)

3) CUSTOMER KA NAAM / NUMBER THEEK KARNA (invoice ban jane ke baad)
   • Orders page -> invoice list -> customer ke naam ke sath ✏️ (pencil) dabayein
   • Naam, number, address theek karein -> Save
   • Admin only. Har change activity log me record hota hai
   • Wohi customer ki saari invoices par naya naam aata hai

4) BACK-DATE INVOICE (sirf ADMIN)
   • Booking form me "🔙 Purani tareekh (backdate)"
   • Bijli/internet band tha aur invoice nahi bani? Purani tareekh daal kar bana lein
   • Khali chhorein = aaj ki tareekh (normal)

5) INVOICE PDF (WhatsApp par bhejne ke liye)
   • Invoice kholein -> naya button "📄 PDF"
   • 📤 Share PDF  -> phone par: WhatsApp chunein -> chat chunein -> send
                     -> PC par: PDF download hogi -> WhatsApp me 📎 se attach karein
   • ⬇️ Download PDF -> file save
   • PDF me dono copies ek hi page par: CUSTOMER COPY + ✂ CUT HERE + OFFICE COPY
     (thermal roll par seedha print bhi ho sakta hai)
   • Internet ki zaroorat nahi — PDF app ke andar hi banti hai

6) TASK BOARD PAR CUSTOMER DETAILS
   • Har card par ab: Invoice # + 👤 Customer naam + 📞 Number
   • 📱 icon se seedha WhatsApp (delivery late hone ki info dene ke liye)
   • "🖨️ Print Tasks" wale printout par bhi naam aur number aata hai

7) ORDERS LIST ME "PAID ON" COLUMN
   • Naya column "Paid On": payment ki tareekh + waqt + "kitne din pehle"
   • Misaal: 12 Nov 2026 / 04:35 PM · 2 din pehle
   • Payment nahi hui to "—"
