AAP KA MASLA
------------
1. Print par wohi invoice 30+ dafa aa rahi thi (33 pages).
2. Invoice ka size chhota aa raha tha / right side cut ho rahi thi.
3. Aap ki bheji hui PDF: 33 pages, har page "Letter" size (612 x 792 pt).

KYA WAJAH THI (PDF khol kar dekhi)
----------------------------------
* Purani settings har copy ke baad zabardasti naya page banati thin AUR andar ke
  elements "absolute position" par thay — isi se Chrome ek hi copy ko baar baar
  chhap deta hai (33 pages).
* Aap ki PDF ka paper "Letter" tha, roll (72mm) nahi — isi liye print chhota aata tha.
* Sab se bari wajah: page ki chaurai 80mm set thi magar paper 72mm — Chrome 8mm
  zyada hissa chhota kar ke POORA print shrink kar deta tha, aur right side kat jati thi.

AB KYA HO GA (fix)
------------------
* Print me HAMESHA sirf 2 pages:  page 1 = CUSTOMER COPY,  page 2 = OFFICE COPY.
  (33 pages, repeat copies — bilkul khatam.)
* Har copy ek hi roll page (297mm) par poori aa jati hai.
* Print ka size 100% — koi chhotapan nahi, koi cut nahi (left/right dono taraf margin barabar).
* Agar invoice bohat bhaari ho (12-15 items) to pehle fuzool khali jagah khatam hoti hai
  (spacing, logo, terms ka size) — text jitna bara mumkin ho. Sirf uske baad font
  halka sa chhota hota hai (100% se 70% tak, us se neeche kabhi nahi).
* Print dialog me paper sirf "72mm x 297mm" dikhega (pehle 594mm jaisa ajeeb size aata tha).
* Logo, invoice number (bara), PIECES (bara), PACKAGING, 9 Terms & Conditions,
  Shop Timings aur "Designed & Developed by: Celinesoft" — sab page 1 par.

CUT LINE (naya)
---------------
* Page 1 (CUSTOMER COPY) me "Celinesoft / Designed & Developed by / Contact" ke
  FORAN baad ab cut line aati hai:

        ✂  - - -  CUT HERE  - - -  ✂
     ==================================

* Page 2 (OFFICE COPY) me bhi bilkul waisi hi cut line (same jagah — credit ke baad).
* Roll wahin se kaat lein — dono copies alag ho jayengi.
* Ye line SIRF PRINT par aati hai. Customer ko WhatsApp/PDF wali copy me cut line
  nahi aati (wo saaf professional copy hai) — jaisa aap ne pehle kaha tha.
* Is se page count nahi barhta — 2 pages hi rehte hain.


NAYI INVOICE LAYOUT (aap ki requests)
-------------------------------------
1. LOGO BARA: brand ka logo ab poori chaurai (72mm) tak bara hota hai
   (pehle 70% tha, ab 100% — height cap 40mm). Har invoice par saaf nazar aayega.

2. BOOKING / DELIVERY DATE: rows ab khuli khuli aur saaf hain (padding barha,
   line-height theek) — pehle "kareeb kareeb" chipke hue thay.

3. ITEMS: ab 3 column hain —
        ITEM                        QTY    AMOUNT
        Shalwar Suit (Press)         3      Rs. 480
        @ Rs. 160
   Qty apne column me, amount apne column me. Rate chhote mein naam ke neeche.

4. DISCOUNT: jab customer ko discount diya ho to saaf dikhta hai —
        DISCOUNT (12.5% off)              − Rs. 130
   Percent bhi, amount bhi. Loyalty discount ho to alag row.
   Discount na ho to ye row aati hi nahi.

5. SHOP TIMINGS: Portal ki setting se (Settings -> Shop Timings) —
   yahan invoice par apne aap chhap jati hai. Portal me badlein, invoice par
   wohi naya timing aayega.

6. TERMS & CONDITIONS: 9 terms ab har invoice par (page 1, timings ke ooper).
   Settings -> Invoice me se band bhi ki ja sakti hain (default ON).

NOTE: Page 1 par sab kuch aata hai — logo, invoice no., PIECES, packaging,
dates, customer, items (item/qty/amount), totals + discount, terms, timings,
credit aur uske foran baad CUT LINE. Page 2 = OFFICE COPY (isi tarah).


LOGO AUR TERMS (nayi tabdeeli)
------------------------------
* BRAND LOGO: pehle se bhi BARA — ab poori roll chaurai (72mm) aur height
  cap 45mm (print par 56mm tak). Har invoice par saaf nazar aata hai.

* TERMS & CONDITIONS: pehle bohat choti thi (7px). Ab **10.5px bold** —
  bilkul "Payment Due" jaisi saaf parhi jati hai. Bhaari invoice par bhi
  sab se chhota size 9.4px se neeche nahi jata (pehle 5.7px tha).

* Jagah kahan se aayi (taake sab ek page par rahe):
    - "CUSTOMER COPY" ab INVOICE NO. ke box ke andar (alag band khatam).
    - Item ka rate "@ Rs. 160" chhote mein naam ke sath.
    - Logo ke neeche tagline/address/phone ki lines chhoti (9.6px).
    - Thank-you line halki chhoti.

* NOTE: Agar aap Settings -> Invoice me "Thank you" line band karein to bhi
  SHOP TIMINGS aur TERMS invoice par aate rahenge (dono alag settings hain).

PRINT KARNE KA TAREEQA (yeh na badlein)
---------------------------------------
1. Invoice kholein  ->  Print button dabayein.
2. Print dialog me:
      Paper size   : aap ka thermal roll (72mm / 80mm — jo setting me hai)
      Margins      : Default / None
      Scale        : 100%   (Fit to page / Shrink NAHI)
      Headers & footers : OFF
3. Print.

SETTINGS (agar size adjust karna ho)
-----------------------------------
Settings  ->  Thermal Print Setup
   * Paper width (mm)   : 72  (80mm roll ke liye)
   * Print ka size      : Normal (auto-fit)  ya  Bara (100% — bilkul chhota nahi)
     - Normal rakhein to upar wala sab khud ho jata hai.
     - Bara rakhein to invoice kabhi chhoti nahi hoti (bohat lambi ho to page 2 chala jata hai).

AGAR PHIR BHI MASLA HO
----------------------
1. Browser me Ctrl + Shift + R  (hard refresh) — service worker naya aana chahiye.
2. Settings me ek dafa "Print ka size = Normal" save karein.
3. Print dialog check karein: Scale 100% ho, Fit-to-page band ho.
4. Phir bhi ajeeb ho to print PDF bana kar bhej dein — hum pages gin kar bata denge.
